# Evaluation results (synthetic, prototype)

Generated by `tools/prototype/evaluate.py`. **These numbers use a simulated acoustic model**
(`tools/prototype/acoustic_sim.py`): they show how the aligner behaves for a given phoneme error
rate and a stated assumption about its confidence scores, not how any real model performs.
Real-model rows will be added when a model is chosen.

Method: the recited phonemes (continuing or stopping reading per word, pauses as silence) are
turned into simulated frames, decoded with greedy CTC, aligned to the expected words with
word-level skip and repeat arcs, and judged per word (`tools/prototype/aligner.py`). A mistake
counts as found when an event of the right kind appears within one word of it. Injected wrong
and added words differ from their neighbours by at least half their phonemes; one-letter
differences are Phase 4 (letter-level) errors.

Seed 1, 150 trials per mistake kind, 300 correct recitations,
ranges of 1 to 3 ayahs. Targets (docs/PLAN.md): false alarms < 2%, mistake recall > 90%.
**Status:** false alarms meet the target (< 2%) at phoneme error rates up to 20% when the model's confidence is informative, and up to 11% otherwise. Recall of the right kind is above 90% only for skipped words; "wrong" and "added" words are found at about 80% (any kind), below the 90% target. Parameters were chosen to favour few false alarms over recall (CLAUDE.md rule 4). Tuning against a real model is still open.

Parameters: `Params(wrong_below=0.5, skip_aligned_max=0.25, low_conf=0.55, min_mismatch=2, repeat_min_ratio=0.75, skip_cost=1.5, repeat_cost=1.0, add_min_tokens=4, add_inside_min=3, max_jump_words=3, pause_frames=8)`.

## Real model: tarteel-ai/whisper-base-ar-quran (task 2.1, first candidate)

`tools/prototype/whisper_eval.py`: first 10 minutes (20 chunks of 30 s) of a professional,
correct recitation of surah 5, transcribed with greedy decoding and compared word by word to the
Tanzil simple-clean text of ayahs 1-60 (diacritics ignored; the model writes plain text).

| Result | Value |
|---|---|
| Words heard / reference words covered | 566 / 559 |
| Word error rate on a **correct** recitation | **10.2%** (26 substituted, 12 dropped, 19 inserted words) |
| Speed on the dev CPU | about 21 s per 30 s chunk (real-time factor 0.7, not streaming) |

Reading: every error here would be a false alarm for Mutqin, against a target below 2%. Errors
cluster where the reciter is fast or a word is rare (for example four consecutive words of
5:3 were replaced by unrelated words); Whisper also cannot see vowel or tajweed errors. It is not
usable as the mistake detector. The phoneme CTC candidates (Zipformer, wav2vec2) are still to be
measured the same way.

## Real model: TBOGamer22/wav2vec2-quran-phonetics (task 2.1, second candidate)

Apache-2.0, not gated, wav2vec2-base (95M parameters, 378 MB in fp32), greedy CTC. Qualitative check
only, on the first minute of the same surah 5 recitation in 15 s chunks. Findings:

- It was trained on **single-word clips** (Buraaq/quran-md-words), not connected recitation. On
  continuous recitation the output is mostly unreadable: the opening words "yā ayyuhā alladhīna
  āmanū awfū bil-ʿuqūd" came out as "bisidahihamhuhiyayhhnānūafūb'naūduḥil-nakūbahmatu...", with
  only fragments right and no word delimiters.
- Its vocabulary is a transliteration (ā ī ū, ʿ, ṭ ...), not our 31 phonemes, so a mapping would be
  needed; its vowels do not mark shaddah or tajweed.
- It is not streaming (full-attention wav2vec2). Speed is good (about 1 s per 15 s on the dev CPU).

Not usable for ayah-level tracking as released. It could only serve for single-word clips.

## Desktop speed (task 3.7, pieces measured; full-model figure pending)

Release build, AMD Ryzen 7 5800H (16 threads), one thread used. Real-time factor (RTF) =
processing time / audio time; lower is faster. Reproduce: `-DMUTQIN_BUILD_BENCH=ON`, run
`mutqin_bench 120`, and `mutqin-cli ... --posteriors FILE --chunk N`.

| Stage | Measurement | RTF |
|---|---|---|
| Log-mel features | 120 s of audio in 0.27 s | 0.0022 |
| Energy VAD | 120 s in 1.6 ms | 0.00001 |
| Aligner + detector, whole 167 s recitation of 2:6-40 (700+ words) pushed at once | 214 ms | 0.0013 |
| same, pushed every 250 ms (668 pushes) | 766 ms total | 0.0046 |
| same, pushed every 40 ms (4,175 pushes) | 2.97 s total, 0.7 ms per push | 0.018 |
| same, pushed every 10 ms (16,699 pushes) | 10.7 s total, 0.64 ms per push | 0.064 |
| sherpa-onnx backend with a public int8 streaming Zipformer CTC test model (Chinese, 5.6 s and 1.8 s clips) | 0.24 s and 0.08 s | 0.04 |

Reading: the front end and the aligner cost almost nothing next to a model; even pushing every
10 ms keeps the aligner far below the 0.3 target. The test model is not our phoneme model (the
Zipformer from the candidate list is 65M parameters, similar in size to this one), so the model
row is indicative only; the real figure and the latency to a reported mistake need the real model.
Phone benchmarks are deferred to Phase 3b.

## Real model: Muno459/zipformer_p-quran (task 2.1, third candidate, first run 2026-10-14)

Streaming Zipformer2 CTC, 65.5M parameters, int8 ONNX (73 MB), run through sherpa-onnx (greedy). Its output
is 250 phoneme units; the aligner here compares the units heard against the model's own canonical
phonemes of each word (`ordered_quran_phonemes.json`, shipped with the model), unchanged aligner and
default parameters (`tools/prototype/zipformer_eval.py`). The recording is a professional, correct
recitation of surah 5 (49 minutes, 120 ayahs, 2,444 words judged), so every flagged word is a false alarm.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Flagged words (wrong or skipped) on correct recitation | **44 of 2,444 = 1.8%** (first 10 minutes: 4 of 487 = 0.8%); target < 2% |
| All events including repeated / added | 83 (3.4% of words): 34 wrong, 30 repeated, 10 skipped, 9 added |
| Speed (4 threads, dev CPU) | real-time factor 0.031 (49 min in 90 s) |
| For comparison, Whisper base on the same file | 10.2% word errors |

What the flags look like: most are the **first word of an ayah** where the first one or two units are
missing or attached to the previous word (expected `يَ اااا ءَ ييُ هَ`, heard `ييُ هَ`), plus a few real
recognition errors. Not yet measured: recall on injected mistakes, other reciters, learners' recitation,
phone microphones. The reciter's madd lengths differ from the model's fixed canonical lengths (4 counts),
so long-vowel units can differ by length without being a mistake.
