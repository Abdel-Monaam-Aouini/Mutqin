## Findings (task 1.10)

**Alignment.** The reference offsets index the Tanzil Uthmani text *without* the waqf signs
(U+06D6 to U+06DB) and the space before each; tatweel, the basmala prefix and the sajdah sign are
kept. About 3.5% of its spans (ikhfa check) are misplaced by a few characters, and spans drift in very
long ayahs (4:23, 33:35, 66:5). "Near" means the same rule within 8 characters.

**Agreement.** The engine finds 99.6% to 100% of the reference places for ghunnah, all idgham and ikhfa
rules, iqlab, madd_2, madd_246, muttasil and qalqalah ("Found" column). Lower rows: madd_munfasil 94.0%
(item 4), lam_shamsiyyah 95.2% (see below), madd_6 70.3% (item 5). The engine's rules also agree with
the written signs in the text (sukun, bare letter, meem sign, maddah), checked in the test suite.

**Differences:**

1. *Label only, same sound.* A noon or meem merged into a doubled letter and written as one word is
   `ghunnah` (mushaddad, spec 3.3) in the engine, `idghaam_ghunnah` / `idghaam_shafawi` in the reference
   (about 2,000 of the engine-only `ghunnah`). Both give 2 counts.
2. *The name of Allah.* The reference does not mark `lam_shamsiyyah` there; the engine does
   (about 2,500 engine-only). Spec 3.5 treats the lam of Allah separately (open question).
3. *Natural madd.* The reference marks natural madd mainly where the text writes a superscript alef
   and only about 10 to 15% of plain alef / waw / yeh madd letters, with no pattern found (about 27,000
   engine-only `madd_2`). The long vowels themselves are not in doubt.
4. *Vocative "ya" and similar words written joined to a hamza word* (188): the reference says
   munfasil, the engine says muttasil because the text writes one token. Both allow 4 or 5 counts;
   only the tag differs. Needs a teacher decision (open question).
5. *Letter names at surah openings* (44 `madd_6`): skipped by the engine until the special-cases
   data (task 1.9) exists.
6. *'Arid before a tanween ending* (about 450 engine-only `madd_246`) and a few qalqalah places
   (about 200 engine-only): the reference does not mark them; to check with the teacher.
7. *Not comparable.* `hamzat_wasl`: the engine emits no item for a silent wasla (only the starting
   form of the article), the reference marks 13,000 places. `silent` letters (4,174) and the two
   idgham-of-similar-letters rules (71) are not modelled yet (spec 3.8 is an open question).

**Not explained yet:** 132 `lam_shamsiyyah` places the reference marks and the engine does not (the
reference marks the lam in positions such as a lam after a vowel-final word; to check with the teacher),
and 3 to 9 reference-only cases in each of the other rules, listed by ayah below.

**Fixed because of this validation:** the merged form of an idgham (doubled letter with ghunnah) now
exists only when the reading goes on from the previous word, not after a stop.
